# Customer Success + AI Operations

**Relevant for:** AI transformation, strategic customer success, implementation, solutions, CSOps/RevOps, customer-facing AI, operations leadership.

## Context

A lot of AI adoption fails for the same reason customer onboarding fails: the workflow is unclear, ownership is fuzzy, and nobody is measuring whether the customer actually reached value.

That is the through-line between my customer success work and my current AI work.

## What I Built Before AI

At Perch Security, I built the customer success function from scratch during startup scaling and acquisition.

The results were measurable:

- 1.7% churn;
- 96%+ CSAT;
- 97% NPS;
- CS team scaled to 12;
- 5 direct reports;
- onboarding, support, health workflows, product feedback loops, customer cadence, and automation.

The work was not magic. It was operating discipline: make customer risk visible, tighten handoffs, create a cadence for action, and keep the team close to what customers were actually experiencing.

## How That Shows Up In AI Work

For Paput.ai / EUCompli.ai, I use the same lens:

- start with the workflow, not the model;
- define who owns the outcome;
- make each step visible;
- preserve evidence;
- add review gates where automation can create risk;
- measure time-to-value and operational load;
- keep support, sales, product, and founder workflows connected.

## Key Decisions

**Do not automate a broken workflow without naming the breakage.**  
If a handoff is unclear, AI will usually make the confusion faster, not better.

**Make AI output reviewable.**  
Structured outputs, evidence, and traceability matter because customers and internal teams need to trust the result.

**Build for adoption, not novelty.**  
The question is not "can we use an agent here?" The question is "will this reduce friction for the person who owns this workflow?"
