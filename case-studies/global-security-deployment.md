# Global Security Deployment Operations

**Relevant for:** security platform leadership, onboarding, implementation, enterprise delivery, customer operations, AI security/compliance platforms.

## Context

Security products do not become valuable when the contract is signed. They become valuable when the customer is deployed, monitored, supported, and able to trust the operating process around the tool.

At ConnectWise, my work sat in that deployment layer.

## Scope

I led global security deployment/onboarding operations across US, EMEA, and APAC.

Representative scope:

- 26-person global security onboarding organization;
- 200+ monthly security projects;
- SIEM, IDS/IPS, EDR/MDR, incident response, and security operations tooling;
- 18-person international IDS deployment/support team;
- readiness checks, deployment playbooks, handoff redesign, escalation paths, and operational automation;
- customer satisfaction, backlog, SLA, risk, and performance reporting to executive leadership and the board.

## What Changed

The work was about making deployment repeatable and visible:

- define readiness before kickoff;
- standardize deployment playbooks;
- redesign handoffs between sales, onboarding, support, and security operations;
- make backlog, SLA, customer risk, and escalation status visible;
- report the right information to executives without burying them in project noise;
- automate repeatable checks without hiding ownership.

## Results

- Reduced customer time-to-value to under 30 days.
- Identified $1.575M in unrealized annual contract value through a billing/contract audit workflow tied to deployed security services.
- Built better regional visibility across project load, customer readiness, escalation risk, and team performance.

## Key Decisions

**Readiness has to come before speed.**  
Fast onboarding does not help if the customer is not technically or operationally ready.

**SLA reporting should create action, not just dashboards.**  
The point of visibility is to move stuck work, surface risk, and make escalation paths obvious.

**Deployment is part of the product.**  
For security and AI platforms, the handoff, evidence, support path, and reporting layer shape the customer experience as much as the software does.
